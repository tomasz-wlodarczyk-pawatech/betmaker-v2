- the user enters desired total_odds (default 10, min 2, max 1000)

- the app searches though the selections that have “hot” equals 1
- the app tries to combine the selections (1 to 50 selections) for the betslip so that total_odds on the multibet match the desired total_odds (+-15%)
- you can’t use selections from the same event more than once on the betslip
- the app generates the proposed betslip with all the relevant info
